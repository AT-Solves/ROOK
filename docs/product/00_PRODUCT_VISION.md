# ROOK — Product Vision

## 1. Product

ROOK is an enterprise AI Chief of Staff that connects a leader's communication, meetings, work systems, decisions, commitments, risks, and follow-ups into one contextual leadership layer.

ROOK does not replace Teams, Slack, email, calendars, Jira, documents, or project-management systems. It connects them and turns fragmented organizational signals into actionable executive intelligence.

## 2. Vision

Make every leader able to understand what matters, why it matters, what changed, what was decided, who owns the next action, what is at risk, and what requires their attention—without manually searching across multiple systems.

## 3. Core Promise

Communication → Context → Decision → Commitment → Action → Follow-up → Outcome.

ROOK preserves continuity across this chain.

## 4. Category

Primary category: AI Chief of Staff.

Broader positioning: AI-powered Leadership Operating System.

## 5. What ROOK Is

- A cross-channel context and execution layer.
- An executive intelligence assistant.
- A decision and commitment memory.
- A proactive follow-up and risk detection system.
- A contextual preparation and briefing system.
- A controlled action assistant.

## 6. What ROOK Is Not

- A generic chatbot.
- Only a meeting summarizer.
- Only an email assistant.
- Only a task manager.
- Only a project dashboard.
- An employee surveillance system.
- An autonomous agent with unrestricted authority.

## 7. Target Users

Primary:
- CEO / Founder
- COO
- Chief of Staff
- VP / Business Leader

Secondary:
- Directors
- Product leaders
- Engineering leaders
- Operations leaders
- Program leaders
- Strategy / PMO leaders

## 8. Core User Questions

ROOK should help answer:

1. What needs my attention?
2. What changed?
3. What decisions are pending?
4. What did we decide?
5. Who committed to what?
6. What am I waiting for?
7. What is at risk?
8. What should I do next?
9. What do I need to know before this meeting?
10. Where did this information come from?

## 9. Differentiator

ROOK's primary differentiation is cross-channel contextual continuity.

Example:

Meeting → email → Slack message → Jira issue → project update → follow-up.

ROOK should recognize that these may be connected parts of the same business event.

## 10. Trust Principle

Important insights must be evidence-backed.

ROOK should expose source, timestamp, context, and confidence where appropriate. If evidence is insufficient, ROOK must say so rather than inventing an answer.

## 11. Human Control

ROOK may observe and recommend automatically. Preparing or executing external actions must respect permissions and configurable approval policies.

## 12. Product Personality

ROOK should feel:
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Precise
- Executive
- Trustworthy
- Proactive
- Discreet
- Non-intrusive

It should not feel noisy, gamified, or like a generic chatbot.

## 13. Product North Star

Important organizational signals successfully converted into timely executive action.

## 14. Long-Term Vision

ROOK becomes the trusted intelligence layer across an organization's communication and execution ecosystem, helping leaders operate with continuity, context, and foresight.
