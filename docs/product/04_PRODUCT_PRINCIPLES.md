# ROOK — Product Principles

1. Context over content.
ROOK should connect information, not merely summarize it.

2. Action over activity.
The product measures useful outcomes, not AI usage.

3. Evidence over confidence theater.
Important claims require traceable support.

4. Permission before intelligence.
Unauthorized information must never enter the user's answer.

5. Human control over autonomous behavior.
ROOK can recommend broadly but executes only within explicit policy.

6. Signal over noise.
ROOK should surface what matters and suppress low-value information.

7. Continuity over snapshots.
A decision should remain connected to its later actions, outcomes, and risks.

8. Calm over urgency.
ROOK must not manufacture urgency.

9. Explicit fact over inference.
The system must distinguish what was stated from what was inferred.

10. Modular by design.
Models, connectors, retrieval systems, and infrastructure should be replaceable.

11. Enterprise from day one.
Security, tenancy, auditability, accessibility, and observability are foundational.

12. Build the smallest valuable system first.
Validate the core ROOK loop before expanding the platform.
